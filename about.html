About the publication

Useful information. No invented evidence.

ServiceStack Guide is an independent editorial research project for owners and small teams evaluating business software, websites, customer inquiry tools and service-business workflows. Our objective is to help readers ask better questions before subscribing to a tool.

How the guides are made

We start with a practical buying decision, use provider documentation and other identifiable sources, summarize differences in plain language and create original worksheets or calculators where they help. Software features and prices may change. Readers should verify important purchase terms with the provider before buying.

Guides may be researched, drafted, or edited with AI assistance. We do not describe a tool as independently tested unless the testing actually occurred and evidence has been recorded. We do not invent customer testimonials, benchmarks or company outcomes.

Commercial relationships

Some future articles may contain affiliate links. If you click a tracked link and buy a qualifying product, this publication may receive a commission; we will identify these relationships where links appear. At launch, the current provider links are ordinary direct links with no affiliate tracking. Editorial recommendations are not a guarantee of suitability, price or results.

Images and examples

Some photographs on this site are original AI-generated editorial illustrations used to explain general business scenarios. They are not evidence of a particular company, customer or product test. Any actual vendor screenshot, when used, must be identified separately with its source and rights.

Privacy and data

At this stage, ServiceStack Guide does not offer a registration or user-account system. Embedded calculators run in your browser, without sending your entries to a ServiceStack server. We do not claim to measure user visits or conversions before an analytics service has actually been connected and disclosed here.

We welcome corrections when source evidence is available. A direct contact channel and full legal publisher details will be added before collecting reader data or entering external commercial arrangements.
