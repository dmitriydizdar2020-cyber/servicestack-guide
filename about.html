THE EDITORIAL DESK / ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Our work begins with a better question.

ServiceStack Journal is an independent research-led publication for people running local service businesses. We translate vendor documentation and everyday operational decisions into practical, understandable guides.

RESEARCH, NOT HYPE.

How the guides are made

We begin with a real decision: selecting a website builder, collecting a quote request, following up on a lead or understanding recurring software costs. We check official documentation, explain meaningful trade-offs and provide original checklists or calculators when useful.

Software plans and prices can change. We encourage readers to confirm important terms directly with providers before purchasing. Our content may be drafted or edited with AI assistance, but we never claim firsthand product testing unless we have documented it.

Commercial relationships

Some future guides may contain affiliate links. If you follow a qualifying tracked link and buy a product, ServiceStack may receive a commission; those links will be clearly disclosed. At present, provider links are direct links without affiliate tracking.

Illustrations and photographs

Some imagery on this site is AI-generated and labeled as editorial illustration. Such imagery is not evidence of real clients, actual company projects, or a product test. Actual vendor screenshots, if added, will be distinguished and sourced.

Privacy and information

The website's planning tools operate within your browser. At this stage, ServiceStack has no member accounts, contact form or connected visitor analytics. Any future personal-data collection will require a corresponding privacy explanation and appropriate controls.

We will add a verified contact channel and legal publisher information before collecting reader information or introducing paid business services.
