## 9/10 graduation criteria (all are required)

1. **Editorial operations:** 30 consecutive local calendar days with reproducible daily production/release ledger, independent QAD for every indexable item, no hidden failures, actual throughput measured (five/day a goal, never a safety bypass).
2. **Content quality:** sample of 20 published pages independently reviewed for correct primary evidence, clear commercial disclosure, non-duplicative utility, accessibility and mobile screenshots; no unresolved critical findings.
3. **SEO and GEO:** index coverage checked in the actual Google Search Console property; clean canonical and sitemaps; observed organic impressions and clicks with an explicit reporting window; topical clusters show real query discovery, not keyword stuffing.
4. **Analytics:** reproducible measurement of organic clicks, supported merchant referrals, approved conversions, reversals and **cash paid**. Unknowns must remain unknown.
5. **Business validation:** at least one actual merchant payout supported by provider statement/receipt; payout country, payment entity and affiliate rules are verified. No conflation of pending vs paid.
6. **Scalability:** repeatable documented publish-and-QA pipeline, zero critical deploy regressions, and at least one measured post-publication improvement to a guide based on real user/search evidence.
7. **Owner control:** no external spend without owner approval, no fabricated specialists, testimonials, product testing or backlinks, clear ability to pause or rollback.

## Guidelines

People-first content and substantive utility take precedence over volume. Google explicitly warns that scaled unoriginal AI-generated pages primarily intended to manipulate search rankings may be spam. There is no guaranteed SEO position or AI-search placement. Use Google Search Central's published guidance on scaled content and AI-assisted content.
