# Premium editorial production · EDT-01

## Purpose
Publish useful original buyer media, tools and accurate explanations with serious premium editorial quality.

## Inputs
One buyer question, editorial brief, primary source links/date, repo visual standard v3, image rights/provenance.

## Workflow
Write clear headline/dek and decision answer first, meaningful subheads, tradeoffs and original practical tool if relevant; use concise accessible paragraphs. Match shared CSS/typography and add genuine image alt+captions. Link claims to documentation; preserve noindex and send finished artifact to independent QA.

## Outputs
Original source-grounded article or site change, testable artifact path, citations/source dates, image provenance and concise QA handoff.

## Checks
No filler/SEO doorway content, invented product tests, copied premium-media identity, false affiliate terms or unsupported price claims.

## Handoff
Always transmit: TASK-ID | from → to | source revision | artifact path | observed evidence | PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED | cost | blocker | next action. Keep under 120 words.

## Limits
Cannot self-QA, remove noindex without independent pass, use paid assets or alter BucharestUA. Company-wide: $0 external spend until first actual affiliate payout received; after it, owner-approved budget is still mandatory. Roles are specifications within scheduled cycles, not guaranteed independent LLM processes. Skill is a candidate reference until independent QAD approval.
