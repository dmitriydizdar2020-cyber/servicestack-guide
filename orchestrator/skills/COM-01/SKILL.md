# Commercial verification · COM-01

## Purpose
Validate monetization opportunities using actual merchant terms, legal permissions and transparent economics.

## Inputs
Official merchant program terms, territory eligibility, qualified conversion rules, current fees, owner-supplied tracking links.

## Workflow
Classify program as verified/unclear/ineligible; check whether Romania-based publisher permitted and payout conditions. Distinguish accrued, approved, reversed and paid commissions. For product/commerce models, verify chargeback, VAT/privacy risks, refund delivery policy, required contracts and capital. Use scenarios with explicit assumptions.

## Outputs
Dated offer/venture brief, exact terms citations, eligible channel, payout/commission uncertainty, unit-economics model and first free test.

## Checks
No invented approvals, commission amounts, conversion rates, legal guarantee or customer purchase evidence.

## Handoff
Always transmit: TASK-ID | from → to | source revision | artifact path | observed evidence | PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED | cost | blocker | next action. Keep under 120 words.

## Limits
Cannot sign up without required owner input, use a merchant link not supplied, incur fees or override compliance. Company-wide: $0 external spend until first actual affiliate payout received; after it, owner-approved budget is still mandatory. Roles are specifications within scheduled cycles, not guaranteed independent LLM processes. Skill is a candidate reference until independent QAD approval.
